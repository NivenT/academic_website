In the latter part of my first year as a postdoc, I tried taking some handbalancing (stable hand stands) classes at a place called [Esh Circus Arts](https://www.eshcircusarts.com/). Admittedly, I am still, what the kids call, "Not that good," but it has been a fun challenge. It turns out, doing a good handstand requires several, easy-to-forget, bodily holds/pushes/squeezes and if you are not doing all of them at the start of your stand, you will probably comoe down to the ground before you have time to remember to engage all your muscles and whatnot. So, until the strength and muscle memory is really built up, it tends to take people a long time before they start having a decently stable handstand.
