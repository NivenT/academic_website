I rarely ever read growing up, except when required to for school. In grad school, one of my friends recommended [N. K. Jemisin's Broken Earth Trilogy](https://nkjemisin.com/series/the-broken-earth/) to me, and I was quite moved by the book. This was a good recommendation, so much so in fact, that it kicked off a "reading phase" of my life (turns out, books are kinda cool. Who knew?). So, to pay his recommendation forward, and to remind myself of books I enjoyed, here are a few books/series I read in grad school/early postdoc which I quite enjoyed. It'd be fun to one day come back and record detailed versions of my thoughts on each, but for now, you probably just get titles.
