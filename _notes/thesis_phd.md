This was my PhD thesis, a version of [the paper of the same name](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08493). It, among other things, shows that, over **any** global function field $$K$$, the average rank of elliptic curves over $$K$$ is bounded. It is a bit different fromo the "official" version of this paper, mostly in that it proves a slightly weaker main result. There is good reason for this though. I spent a long time in grad school working out some computations (what became Section 2.1 of this thesis) which ended up not being strong enough to imply the main result I was after and so were scrapped from the "official" version. Since I spended all that time working them out, I did not want them to go to waste, so I chucked them into my thesis at the cost of weakening its main statement.
