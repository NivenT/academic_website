[Santiago Arango-Piñeros](https://sarangop1728.github.io/), [Sameera Vemulapalli](https://sites.google.com/view/sameeravemulapalli/home), and I have a fun [new paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31699) out. We count primitive integral solutions to so-called Euclidean (generalized) Fermat equations, i.e. equations of the form $$F\colon Ax^a+By^b+Cz^c=0$$, where $$1/a+1/b+1/c=1$$. Such equations have an associated genus 1 curve $$C$$, and the heart of our work is teasing out the difference between "rational points" on $$C$$ and "primitive integral solutions" to $$F$$.
