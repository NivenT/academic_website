[Katy Woo](https://khwoo.math.ethz.ch/) and I just posted an exciting [new paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16062). The paper proves a generalization of Green-Sawhney's $$p^2+nq^2$$ result, and then uses this to construct rank 2 elliptic curves in quadratic twist families and to deduce new successful instances of the fibration method for studying the Brauer-Manin obstruction on varieties fibered over $$\P^1$$ (e.g. on conic bundles).
